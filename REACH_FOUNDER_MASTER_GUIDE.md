## 2. Core Platform Features & How They Work

### 📞 1. Native 1-on-1 Voice & Video Calling (WhatsApp Style)
* **Architecture**: 100% native WebRTC audio and video streams inside direct chats (zero external meeting links).
* **Experience**: User A calls User B $\rightarrow$ User B's screen rings with an incoming call overlay. When answered, high-definition audio/video starts with picture-in-picture local camera tiles, microphone/camera toggles, and live call timers.
* **Synchronized Teardown**: When either party ends the call, both sides are automatically disconnected.

### 📜 2. Verified Deal Room & 1-Click YC SAFE Note Generator
* **Pipeline Stages**: Structured deal execution stages:
  1. **NDA Stage**: Mutual confidentiality before data room access.
  2. **Term Sheet Stage**: Valuation cap, discount rate, and check size negotiation.
  3. **Agreement Stage**: Standard Y-Combinator Post-Money SAFE note generated with digital verification stamps.
  4. **Closed Stage**: Deal finalized with post-closing invoices and milestone records.
* **1-Click PDF Export**: Clean, printable document layout (`window.print()`) formatted for venture capital legal standards.

### 👁️ 3. Confidential Pitch Deck Watermarking & Investor Telemetry
* **Dynamic Watermarking**: Stamped with the viewing investor's full name, email, and timestamp across every slide.
* **Real-Time Founder Alerts**: As soon as an investor opens a deck, the founder receives an instant notification:
  > *"🔔 Pitch Deck Inspected: Investor [Name] just reviewed your confidential Pitch Deck in the Verified Deal Room."*

### 🛡️ 4. Identity KYC & Automated Trust Scores
* **Verification Engine**: Multi-tiered identity audit verifying national ID cards, passports, and corporate registration documents.
* **Trust Score (0–100)**: Displays public verification badges (`✓ Verified Member`) to eliminate scam activity and build high investor conviction.

### 🚀 5. Viral Pre-Launch Waitlist Engine (`/waitlist`)
* **Role-Specific Onboarding**: Separate qualification questionnaires for Investors (ticket size: \$5k–\$500k+), Founders (target raise), and Talent.
* **Live Queue Counter**: Displays real-time VIP line placement (*"You're #142 in line"*).
* **Viral Referral Loop**: Users get unique invite links (`/waitlist?ref=RCH-XXXXXX`) to jump 10 spots for every 3 peers invited.

## 4. Actionable Investor Acquisition Playbook (5 Zero-Spend Tactics)

### 🎯 Tactic 1: The "Curated Deal Flow" Cold Message (LinkedIn / X)
* **The Rule**: Never pitch the platform itself. **Pitch 1 exciting startup raising capital.**
* **The Script to Send Angels**:
  > *"Hi [Name], saw your angel investments in [Fintech/AI]. We just verified an emerging market payments startup on REACH raising a \$250k seed round with \$18k MRR and 40% MoM growth. Here is a private VIP pass to review their watermarked pitch deck and data room: [Your-Waitlist-Link]"*
* **Why it works**: Angels ignore generic platform pitches, but they **always** open vetted deals in their niche.

### 🌍 Tactic 2: Tap Diaspora Angel Communities & WhatsApp/Telegram Groups
* **Target Audience**: High-earning tech professionals (Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Stripe) living in the US, UK, Canada, and UAE who want to invest \$5k–\$25k in emerging startups back home.
* **Value Proposition**: REACH gives them KYC-vetted startups, legal SAFE contracts, and direct 1-on-1 founder calls without requiring them to travel.

### 🔄 Tactic 3: Turn Startup Founders into Investor Inviters (Viral Loop)
* **Instruction to Founders**: Tell every onboarded founder to host their fundraising round on REACH:
  > *"Share your REACH Deal Room link with all your existing angels so they can sign your SAFE agreement and review your watermarked deck in 1 click."*
* **Result**: When 10 founders invite their private angel network, you acquire **50+ investors with zero marketing effort**.

### 🏛️ Tactic 4: Partner with Accelerators & University Incubators
* **The Offer**: Provide local startup accelerators with a private digital Deal Room for their demo day cohorts.
* **Why they accept**: Accelerators struggle to connect their founders with international investors. REACH provides the digital infrastructure.

### 🎤 Tactic 5: Host a 45-Minute "REACH Virtual Pitch Night"
* **Format**: Invite 15–20 angel investors to a private 45-minute live pitch session using REACH's native video engine.
* **Flow**: 3 startups pitch for 5 minutes each + 5 minutes Q&A. Immediately after the call, investors click into the Deal Room to initiate term sheets.
