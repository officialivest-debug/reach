2. Core Features & How They Operate

📞 Native 1-on-1 Voice & Video Calling (WhatsApp Style)

100% native peer-to-peer WebRTC video and audio inside direct chat. When User A clicks Call, User B’s screen immediately rings with an answer/decline modal. Video includes floating picture-in-picture local camera tiles, mute controls, and synchronized two-way teardown.

📜 Verified Deal Room & 1-Click YC SAFE Note Generator

Structured pipeline tracking across 4 venture stages: NDA → Term Sheet → Agreement → Closed. Generates industry-standard Y-Combinator Post-Money SAFE agreements with Valuation Cap, Discount Rate, Pro-Rata terms, and 1-click printable PDF formatting.

👁️ Confidential Pitch Deck Watermark Viewer & Real-Time Alerts

Dynamically applies a personalized watermark (investor name, email, date) across every slide. Whenever an investor inspects a deck, an instant real-time notification alerts the founder: "Investor [Name] just reviewed your confidential Pitch Deck in the Deal Room."

🛡️ Verified KYC & Trust Scoring

Verifies government ID cards, passports, and corporate registrations to award public verified badges and compute trust scores (0–100), eliminating scam activity.

🚀 Viral Pre-Launch Waitlist System (/waitlist)

Collects role-specific registrations (ticket size for investors, target raise for founders) with live queue position counters (#142 in line) and viral referral links (invite 3 peers to jump 10 spots).

4. Actionable Investor Acquisition Playbook (5 Zero-Spend Tactics)

Tactic 1 · Highest Response (~28%)

The "Curated Deal Flow" Cold Message (LinkedIn / X)

Never pitch the platform itself. Pitch 1 specific exciting startup deal raising capital in the investor's niche.

"Hi [Name], saw your angel investments in Fintech. We just verified an emerging payments startup on REACH raising a $250k seed round with $18k MRR and 40% MoM growth. Here is a private VIP pass to review their watermarked deck and metrics in the Deal Room: [Your-Waitlist-Link]"
Tactic 2 · High Check Sizes

Diaspora Angel Networks (US, UK, Canada, UAE)

Target diaspora tech professionals (Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Stripe) who want to invest $5k–$25k in emerging startups back home. REACH provides them with verified KYC, legal SAFE contracts, and direct video calls.

Tactic 3 · Viral Founder Referrals

Turn Onboarded Startup Founders into Investor Inviters

Instruct your 10 onboarded founders to send their REACH Deal Room link to their existing angels so investors sign their SAFE notes in 1-click. 10 founders bring in 50+ investors with zero work from you.

Tactic 4 · Strategic Partnerships

Partner with Accelerators & University Incubators

Offer pre-seed accelerators a free digital Deal Room for their demo day cohorts, bringing all their founders and attending angel investors directly onto REACH.

Tactic 5 · Fast Activation

Host a 45-Minute "Virtual Pitch Showcase"

Invite 15 angel investors to a 45-minute live demo session on REACH native video calling. 3 startups pitch for 5 mins each. Immediately after, angels open the Deal Room and initiate term sheets.
